What "Free 100" Really Means in the Philippine Market

  • Deposit match (100% up to ₱X): You deposit ₱100 and the casino matches it, giving you ₱200 total to play with. This is the most common "free 100" in the market.

How to Evaluate a Free 100 Bonus Before You Claim

Experienced Filipino players run every offer through the same checklist. If a promo fails more than one or two of these tests, it is usually not worth your time.
